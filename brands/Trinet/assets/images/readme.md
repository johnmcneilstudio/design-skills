# TriNet image references

This folder contains selected references for the current JMS photography direction for TriNet. It is a **style-of collection**, not a complete image library or a list of approved production assets.

Images may be added, replaced or removed as the work evolves. Use the shared visual qualities below as the source of truth rather than relying on any individual filename or subject.

## Visual direction

Photography should feel candid, immediate and grounded in the reality of running a business.

Look for:

- Real small-business owners, employees and teams in authentic workplaces
- People actively working, making, serving, preparing, moving or collaborating
- Natural interaction rather than posed performance
- Documentary or editorial framing with a sense of being inside the moment
- Natural light or direct flash, with strong contrast and believable color
- Imperfect, human details: movement, gestures, partial crops and occasional motion blur
- Visible environmental context that helps explain the business
- A broad mix of people, industries, ages and working situations

Not every subject needs to be at a desk or using a computer. Shops, kitchens, studios, workshops, laboratories, offices, vehicles and service environments can all feel appropriate when the activity is specific and believable.

## Composition

- Favor wide images that can support responsive web, social and video crops.
- Keep enough environment to establish context.
- Use closer portraits when the person and expression carry the story.
- Leave usable negative space when typography needs to share the frame.
- Let images become large when they provide the emotional center of a layout.
- Preserve natural perspective, body proportions and spatial relationships.

## Avoid

- Generic corporate stock photography
- Staged handshakes, meetings or group poses
- Forced smiles and people looking directly at the camera without purpose
- Sterile, overly perfect offices
- Laptop-only imagery with no meaningful story
- Heavy retouching, artificial color washes or excessive effects
- Tight crops that remove all evidence of the workplace
- Polished lifestyle imagery that feels disconnected from real business activity
- Visible AI artifacts, distorted anatomy or implausible environments

## Motion

For video and motion work, carry the same attitude into the footage: handheld or lightly observational, mid-task and grounded in real-world texture. Movement, quick details and imperfect framing are welcome when they make the work feel more immediate and true.

## Using these references

These files define an attitude, not a set of compositions to copy. New photography, licensed stock and generated concepts should match the overall realism, energy, lighting and human specificity without recreating a reference image exactly.

Before production use, confirm the source, licensing and approval status of each image. Record whether an asset is client-supplied, licensed, commissioned, generated or edited from another source.
